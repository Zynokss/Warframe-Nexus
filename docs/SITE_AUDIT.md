## Legal and monetization preparation

- **Warframe IP:** passive non-obstructive ads are permitted by the [DE content policy](https://www.warframe.com/en/contentpolicy); logo use needs express written consent and other creators' fan art needs permission. Add a visible independent/unaffiliated fan-site notice and ownership/source credits. Generated artwork remains Warframe-inspired, not official DE artwork. The policy does not explicitly approve website donations; keep support voluntary, untied to content/IP sales, and seek clarification if attaching perks. Its video donation provisions are not automatic website permission.
- **Morocco:** map data actually processed: server/Cloudflare logs, ad identifiers, donation information and recipients. The [CNDP website guide](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/CNDP-guide-conformite-sites-web-fr.pdf) describes notification, information/security/retention duties and consent for personal-data cookies. Assess applicable formalities before adding collection; never fabricate a CNDP registration number. Check [cross-border transfer procedures](https://www.cndp.ma/transfert-de-donnees-a-letranger/) for overseas hosting/providers. An appropriately qualified local adviser should resolve the exact obligations and revenue/business/tax treatment.
- **Privacy:** disclose operator/contact, purposes, recipients, retention, applicable rights and requests channel. Browser-local preferences are not server accounts; Cloudflare and third-party API/CDN requests still exist. Avoid blanket claims that no data is processed. Offer a reset-local-settings action without clearing unrelated browser data.
- **Ads:** decide the provider before writing the final privacy/consent disclosures. Gate non-exempt ad/analytics storage and scripts as applicable; support reject/manage/withdraw choices and prevent ad clicks from resembling app controls. UK rules can cover localStorage too, with purpose-specific exceptions: [ICO guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-storage-and-access-technologies/), [exceptions](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-the-exceptions/). Functional preferences do not automatically justify a blanket cookie banner. If choosing AdSense/Ad Manager, check the [Google-certified CMP requirement for EEA/UK/Swiss traffic](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13554020?hl=en). Provider policies may also address minors.
- **Donations:** use a hosted payment flow once selected; explain the operator receives support, what it funds, one-time versus recurring charging and cancellation/refund/contact handling. Do not imply DE endorsement, charitable status or tax deductibility without a basis. Provider suitability, Moroccan payouts and actual account/fee terms remain unverified.
- **Licenses:** record original/generated assets separately from DE/creator-owned material. Preserve required third-party notices; the [WFCD item project's MIT license](https://github.com/WFCD/warframe-items/blob/master/LICENSE) does not grant ownership of Warframe art. No repository code license was found; choosing one matters if publishing source, not as permission to relicense third-party IP.
